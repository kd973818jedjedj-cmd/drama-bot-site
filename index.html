drama-bot

drama-bot is a personal automation tool. It generates short vertical story videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

What the app does

  • Creates short videos (script, images, voice-over, subtitles) on a schedule.
  • Uploads each video to the owner's own YouTube channel, marked as AI-generated content.
  • Reads the owner's recent uploads on that channel to avoid uploading the same video twice.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the project. The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.
