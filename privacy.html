Privacy Policy

drama-bot is a personal tool used by a single owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses these Google OAuth scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the list of the owner's recent uploads so a video is never uploaded twice.

How data is used and stored

  • The app does not collect personal data from anyone other than the owner.
  • The OAuth refresh token is stored only as an encrypted secret in the owner's private code repository.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Generated videos are deleted from the processing environment after they are uploaded.

Google and YouTube policies

Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
